The Custom Methodology

How Your Space Takes Form

We don’t believe in one-size-fits-all catalogues. Every bespoke piece follows a precise four-phase journey from floor measurement to installation.

01

Spatial Laser Scan

Our styling specialists visit your Chattogram home or office to capture millimeter-accurate floor dimensions, natural lighting angles, and room circulation paths.

02

Material Curation

Select seasoned timber grades (Burmese Teak, Chittagong Gamari, Walnut), stain tones, and stain-resistant European upholstery swatches at our Agrabad showroom.

03

Master Joinery

In-house artisans carve, assemble mortise-and-tenon joints, apply natural oil finishes, and calibrate ergonomic cushioning over 3 to 4 dedicated weeks.

04

White-Glove Placement

Our internal delivery crew executes full assembly, precise leveling, anti-scratch floor padding, and takes away all wrapping materials cleanly.

Have your own interior design drawings or architectural blueprint?
